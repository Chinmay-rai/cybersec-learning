## What I Understood
- Splunk is a SIEM and data analysis platform used to search, analyze, and visualize large amounts of machine-generated data
- Searches help identify relevant events from large datasets
- Commands extend the functionality of searches and data analysis
- Knowledge Objects help organize and reuse searches, reports, and visualizations
- Dashboards provide a visual way to monitor and analyze data
