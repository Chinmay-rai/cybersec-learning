## What I Understood

- SOC Level 1 analysts primarily monitor, triage, and escalate security alerts
- SOC Level 2 analysts perform deeper investigations and handle more complex incidents
- Threat hunting and detection engineering help improve an organization's overall security posture
- Root cause analysis focuses on identifying why an incident occurred rather than only resolving the immediate alert
- Communication, documentation, collaboration, and critical thinking are essential skills alongside technical expertise
