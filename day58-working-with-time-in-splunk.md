## What I Understood

Time is one of the most important aspects of log analysis. Almost every investigation relies on narrowing down events within a specific time window. Understanding how Splunk processes timestamps, formats time values, and handles different time zones helps improve the accuracy and efficiency of searches.

## Key Takeaways

- Understanding time-related SPL features is a fundamental skill for efficient security investigations.
